Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 24, 2025  ·  App: War of Flags

This Privacy Policy describes how War of Flags ("the App") handles information when you use it. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

1. No Personal Data Collected

The App does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. We do not ask for your name, email address, location, or any other identifying information.

2. No Account Required

The App does not require you to create an account or sign in with any service.

3. No Analytics or Tracking

The App does not use any analytics, crash-reporting, or advertising SDKs. We do not track your behavior inside or outside the App.

4. Internet Permission

The App requests the INTERNET permission solely to stream background music files hosted on our server. No user data is sent during this process.

5. Data Stored on Your Device

Game progress (save files, audio preferences) is stored locally on your device only. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us.

6. Children's Privacy

The App does not collect any data from any user, including children under the age of 13. It is safe for all ages.

7. Changes to This Policy

If we ever update this policy, the new version will be posted at this page with a revised date. Continued use of the App after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
